Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This policy covers the Anurag on AI website (this site) and the AnuragOnAI Publisher web app at /publisher/ ("the app"). Both are operated by Anurag Dikshit (contact: aiautomationofficial27@gmail.com).

The website

The website has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and sets no cookies. Pages are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Video thumbnails load from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com). A YouTube player is loaded only when you press play on a featured video, from youtube-nocookie.com; YouTube's own privacy policy then applies. Links to YouTube, TikTok and other platforms take you to those services.

AnuragOnAI Publisher and TikTok

When you sign in with TikTok, TikTok asks you to approve these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, shown in the app so you can see which account you are posting to.
  • video.publish: to post the video you choose to your account with the caption and settings you choose.
  • video.upload: when you pick "Send to TikTok drafts", to upload the video to your TikTok inbox so you can edit and post it in the TikTok app.

The app also reads your posting options from TikTok (allowed privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are available, the maximum video length) each time you open the post page.

What is stored: your TikTok access and refresh tokens and open ID, on the app's server, in a file only the server can read. Your browser keeps only a random session identifier. Videos pass through the server in parts on their way to TikTok and are not kept after each part is sent. Captions and settings are sent to TikTok and not stored by the app.

What is not done: the app does not sell, share or transfer your data, has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and never posts anything you did not submit yourself.

Deleting your data: press "Sign out" in the app. This revokes the app's TikTok token and deletes your session from the server. You can also remove the app from TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, or email us to have any remaining record deleted. Sessions that are not used expire automatically when TikTok's refresh token expires.

YouTube (owner's channel only)

The operator also uses the app to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel, Anurag on AI, using the YouTube Data API scopes youtube and youtube.upload. That sign-in token is stored only on the operator's own computer and server. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The website and the app are not directed at children under 13, and the app is only for people who can hold a TikTok account under TikTok's own rules.
