Post your finished videos to TikTok from the web

AnuragOnAI Publisher is a web app for creators. Sign in with TikTok, pick a finished video, write the caption, choose who can view it and which interactions to allow, add any commercial-content disclosure, and post it to your TikTok account. You see the upload progress and the post status as TikTok processes it.

Open AnuragOnAI Publisher

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok. You approve access on TikTok's own page (Login Kit). The app asks only for your basic profile and permission to upload and post videos you choose.
  2. Compose. The app shows your TikTok nickname and avatar, a preview of your video, and the privacy options TikTok offers your account right now. Nothing is pre-selected: you choose the privacy level and turn on comments, Duet and Stitch yourself.
  3. Disclose. If the video promotes your own brand or a third party, turn on the commercial content disclosure so TikTok labels it.
  4. Post. Nothing is sent until you press "Post to TikTok". The app uploads the video, shows the progress, and reports when TikTok has published it.
  5. Or send it to your drafts. Prefer to finish in the TikTok app? Choose "Send to TikTok drafts": the video lands in your TikTok inbox and you add the caption, edit and post it there.

Who runs it

AnuragOnAI Publisher is built and operated by Anurag Dikshit, the creator behind Anurag on AI (YouTube, TikTok @anuragonai). It is used to publish the channel's AI explainer videos and is open to any TikTok creator who wants a simple, transparent way to post from a desktop browser.
