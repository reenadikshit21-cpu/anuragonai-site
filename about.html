About Anurag on AI

Twenty years building and shipping products at Google, Asus, Foxconn, Edimax and Wabtec, most recently as an AI Project Manager. Now I check AI claims with real math.

Most AI content is either hype or homework. I spent two decades running programs where things had to ship, on time, to people who noticed when they didn't. I apply the same filter to AI: does the claim survive contact with real numbers and real work, or does it only look good in a demo?

What you'll find

  • Big claims, checked. A company announces a number; we work out whether it adds up (series).
  • AI news in plain language. Filings, launches and benchmarks explained, with what is known kept apart from what is not.
  • AI from Zero. A beginner course that starts from nothing and uses no jargon.
  • Shorts. One number or one claim in under a minute, on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
