How we check a claim

Most AI news repeats a number. We try to find out whether the number can be true.

  1. Find the original. The company post, the filing, the paper or the benchmark page, not a summary of it. When a site blocks automated reading, we read it in a normal browser.
  2. Tag every input. Each number is marked claimed (the company said it), reported (one outlet said it) or assumption (our own estimate, stated as one).
  3. Do the arithmetic in the open. Physics and finance checks use simple formulas you can redo with a calculator. For the bigger stories we publish the worksheet and the script.
  4. Say what would settle it. Every check ends with the measurement or disclosure that would prove it right or wrong.
  5. Label uncertainty and theories. When a cause has not been published, we say so and label any theory as a theory.
